# Code of conduct

Mend is a friendly place for anyone who wants to make audio repair better
and freely available, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback about
the work rather than the person, and help newcomers find their feet.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language,
personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, or sustained
disruption of discussions.

**If something goes wrong:** contact the maintainers privately (see the
repository's profile for contact details). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove comments, code or issues, and may block people who
don't follow this code, in the project's issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
