Mend @VERSION@

A free, open-source audio repair suite: the Mend Editor, a standalone app for each tool, and the tools as Audio Unit, VST3, CLAP and LV2 plugins.

On the next pages you can read the licence and choose which parts to install. Click "Customise" on the Installation Type page to pick, for example, only the VST3 plugins.

Plugins are installed for every user of this Mac, in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. The editor and the standalone apps go in /Applications/Mend.
